Notes from the bar

Taste more in your next cup.

What does “specialty coffee” really mean?

It begins with higher-quality coffee and continues through careful roasting and brewing. For you, it means a cleaner cup with more sweetness, character, and balance.

Why choose a pour-over?

Pour-over gives the barista precise control over extraction. The slower process can reveal delicate flavors and a remarkably clear finish.

What should I order if I usually like lattes?

Start with a familiar favorite or try a house specialty such as our Biscoff Latte. We can help you find the right balance of espresso, milk, and sweetness.

Is there more than coffee?

Absolutely. Coffee Nutz also serves premium matcha, creative seasonal drinks, breakfast plates, sandwiches, and café desserts.
