Our story

Coffee craft without shortcuts.

We work with carefully selected coffees rated 85+ by Specialty Coffee Association standards. Our baristas dial in daily, tasting and adjusting so each espresso, pour-over, and siphon brew reflects the best expression of the coffee.

But Coffee Nutz is not only about caffeine. It is a place to slow down, meet a neighbor, share a thoughtful meal, or turn a quick pickup into a better part of your day.
