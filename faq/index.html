Before your visit

Coffee Nutz frequently asked questions

Learn what we brew, what we serve, and what to expect at our neighborhood coffee shop at 6951 South Custer Road in McKinney, Texas.

Where is Coffee Nutz located?

Coffee Nutz is at 6951 South Custer Road, Suite 500, McKinney, TX 75070, near the Frisco and Plano borders. Storefront parking is available.

Why Coffee Nutz?

Because we offer more than a typical coffee run — specialty coffee, fresh breakfast and lunch, desserts, and a warm neighborhood atmosphere, all in one place.

What specialty coffee do you serve?

We serve specialty coffee from renowned origins such as Panama, Colombia, and Ethiopia, roasted by exceptional partners from Seattle, Dubai, and Texas. We also offer pour over and siphon brewing to showcase the pure, original character of these premium coffee beans.

Best drinks for first-time specialty coffee drinkers

We encourage guests to speak with our baristas about their taste preferences first. Whether you enjoy nutty, chocolatey, fruity, floral, bright, or tea-like notes, our baristas can recommend a coffee bean and brewing method that best matches what you like.

What is pour over coffee?

Pour over coffee is a more intentional brewing method that highlights the original character of specialty coffee beans. Unlike standard drip coffee or regular black coffee, pour over is often made with single origin beans so you can taste the unique flavor profile of the coffee more clearly. The result is usually a cleaner, brighter, and more expressive cup.

What is siphon coffee?

Siphon coffee is a specialty brewing method that produces a clean, aromatic, and silky cup. Compared with pour over, which often emphasizes clarity and a lighter, more direct expression of the bean, siphon coffee is known for a more fragrant, rounded, and refined drinking experience. It is often chosen by guests who want to explore premium single origin coffee in a more distinctive way.

Do you have matcha drinks in McKinney?

Yes. At Coffee Nutz, we serve premium matcha drinks made with premium-grade matcha sourced directly from Japan. Compared with lower-grade matcha, it has a more authentic taste and a smoother, less bitter finish, even without added sweetener.

What food do you serve at Coffee Nutz?

We serve freshly made breakfast and lunch items, including avocado toast, breakfast melts, and other café favorites. Everything is prepared in the Coffee Nutz kitchen with a focus on freshness, balance, and flavor. We care about offering a healthier café experience, with food that is less oily, less salty, and thoughtfully made to feel satisfying without being too heavy.

What desserts do you serve at Coffee Nutz?

At Coffee Nutz, we serve a selection of specialty café desserts including tiramisu, canelé, and Pastel de Nata. These desserts pair perfectly with espresso, pour over, siphon coffee, and matcha, making them a favorite choice for guests looking for coffee and dessert in McKinney.

Do you have seating / Wi-Fi?

Yes. We offer more than 20 seats and free Wi-Fi, making Coffee Nutz a comfortable place to relax, meet with friends, or get some work done.

Do you host private events?

Yes. We host private events and welcome inquiries for gatherings, celebrations, and community occasions. Call (469) 678-5050 for availability and details.
