Family owned · McKinney made

Great coffee should be honest, thoughtful, and shared

Coffee Nutz is a family-owned coffee shop in McKinney for people who want more from their daily cup. We serve thoughtfully crafted specialty coffee, premium matcha, fresh breakfast, lunch, and café desserts in a space made for slow mornings, quick pickups, and everything between.

Made for Your Everyday Routine

Fast enough for busy mornings and comfortable enough for slow afternoons. Coffee Nutz is your South Custer Road stop for coffee, breakfast, lunch, free Wi-Fi, and a better daily ritual.

Specialty Coffee, No Shortcuts

We work with carefully selected coffees rated 85+ by Specialty Coffee Association standards. Our baristas dial in daily and prepare balanced espresso, pour-over, siphon coffee, and seasonal discoveries with care.
