About us

More Than Just Coffee

Coffee Nutz is built for people who want more from their daily coffee.
We serve thoughtfully crafted specialty coffee, quality matcha, and fresh breakfast in a space made for slow mornings, quick pick-ups, and everything in between. From espresso drinks to rotating single-origin coffees, every cup is selected and prepared with care.

Made for Your Everyday Routine

Fast enough for busy mornings. Comfortable enough for slow afternoons. Coffee Nutz is your neighborhood stop for coffee, breakfast, and a better daily ritual.

Specialty Coffee, No Shortcuts

We work with quality beans and focus on balanced, well-made drinks—from classic espresso to seasonal coffee discoveries and pour-overs.
